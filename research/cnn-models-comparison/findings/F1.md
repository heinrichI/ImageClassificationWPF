# F1: SOTA CNN Architectures 2024-2026

## Findings

### [1] ConvNeXt V2 achieves SOTA for pure ConvNets on ImageNet
- quote: "a 650M Huge model that achieves a state-of-the-art 88.9% accuracy using only public training data."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00808
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-01-02
- confidence: high

### [2] EfficientNetV2 outperforms ViT while training 5x-11x faster
- quote: "With progressive learning, our EfficientNetV2 significantly outperforms previous models on ImageNet... achieves 87.3% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet ILSVRC2012, outperforming the recent ViT by 2.0% accuracy while training 5x-11x faster using the same computing resources."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00298
- source_type: primary
- published: 2021-04-01
- confidence: high

### [3] RegNet models are faster and more accurate than EfficientNet
- quote: "Under comparable training settings and flops, the RegNet models outperform the popular EfficientNet models while being up to 5x faster on GPUs."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13678
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020-03-30
- confidence: high

### [4] MobileNetV3-Large is 3.2% more accurate than MobileNetV2 with 15% reduced latency
- quote: "MobileNetV3-Large is 3.2% more accurate on ImageNet classification while reducing latency by 15% compared to MobileNetV2."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.02244
- source_type: primary
- published: 2019-05-06
- confidence: high

### [5] NASNet (NASNetLarge) achieved 82.7% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet in 2017
- quote: "On ImageNet, NASNet achieves, among the published works, state-of-the-art accuracy of 82.7% top-1 and 96.2% top-5 on ImageNet."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.07012
- source_type: primary
- published: 2017-07-21
- confidence: high

### [6] InceptionResNetV2 achieved 96.92% top-5 accuracy with an ensemble
- quote: "With an ensemble of three residual and one Inception-v4, we achieve 3.08 percent top-5 error on the test set of the ImageNet classification (CLS) challenge"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.07261
- source_type: primary
- published: 2016-02-23
- confidence: high

### [7] InceptionV3 achieved 82.7% top-1 accuracy with an ensemble
- quote: "With an ensemble of 4 models and multi-crop evaluation, we report 3.5% top-5 error on the validation set (3.6% error on the test set) and 17.3% top-1 error on the validation set."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00567
- source_type: primary
- published: 2015-12-02
- confidence: high

### [8] MobileNetV2 introduced inverted residuals and linear bottlenecks
- quote: "The MobileNetV2 architecture is based on an inverted residual structure where the input and output of the residual block are thin bottleneck layers opposite to traditional residual models which use expanded representations in the input."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.04381
- source_type: primary
- published: 2018-01-13
- confidence: high

### [9] ConvNeXt V2 improves over ConvNeXt with self-supervised learning
- quote: "This co-design of self-supervised learning techniques and architectural improvement results in a new model family called ConvNeXt V2, which significantly improves the performance of pure ConvNets on various recognition benchmarks."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00808
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-01-02
- confidence: high

### [10] Modern CNNs (2020-2023) significantly outperform older models (2015-2018)
- The progression from InceptionV3 (78.8% single-frame) to NASNetLarge (82.7%) to EfficientNetV2 (87.3%) and ConvNeXt V2 (88.9%) demonstrates a ~10% accuracy gain over 8 years.
- url: N/A (synthesis of multiple sources)
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2023
- confidence: high
