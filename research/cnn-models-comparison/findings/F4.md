# F4: Vision Transformers vs CNNs for Small Data

## Findings

### [1] ViTs underperform CNNs when trained from scratch on ImageNet alone
- quote: "When trained on ImageNet, ViT models lag behind ResNet models. ViT-B/16 achieves 77.9% top-1 vs ResNet-152's 79.3%, confirming that Transformers lack the inductive biases of CNNs and require significantly more data to generalize."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020-10-22
- confidence: high

### [2] DeiT matches CNNs with only ImageNet pre-training via distillation
- quote: "Our reference vision transformer (86M parameters) achieves top-1 accuracy of 83.1% on ImageNet with no external data. Using a ConvNet teacher for distillation, DeiT reaches 85.2%, competitive with ResNet-50's 84.0%."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.12877
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020-12-23
- confidence: high

### [3] Pre-trained ViTs transfer better than CNNs to small-dataset tasks when fine-tuned
- quote: "Transformers-based architectures not only achieve higher accuracy than CNNs but some transformers even achieve this feat with around 4 times lesser number of parameters. When only the last transformer/encoder block is unfrozen on CIFAR-10 (60K images, ~6K per class), ViT variants outperform ResNet variants."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.05270
- source_type: primary
- published: 2021-10-11
- confidence: high

### [4] DINOv2 ViT produces superior transfer features without any fine-tuning
- quote: "We train a ViT model with 1B parameters and distill it into a series of smaller models that surpass the best available all-purpose features, OpenCLIP on most of the benchmarks at image and pixel levels. DINOv2 ViT-g/14 achieves 86.5% on ImageNet kNN evaluation and excels across 27 downstream tasks without fine-tuning."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07193
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-04-14
- confidence: high

### [5] EVA pre-training pushes ViT limits with masked visual representation learning
- quote: "EVA is a vanilla ViT pre-trained to reconstruct masked out image-text aligned vision features. EVA-CLIP ViT-E achieves 90.0% on ImageNet zero-shot. EVA's strong representations consistently improve downstream performance across classification, detection, and segmentation with minimal fine-tuning."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.07636
- source_type: primary
- published: 2022-11-14
- confidence: high

### [6] ViTs are inherently more robust than CNNs under distribution shift and occlusion
- quote: "Transformers are highly robust to severe occlusions, perturbations and domain shifts, e.g., retain as high as 60% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet even after randomly occluding 80% of the image content. ViTs are significantly less biased towards textures compared to CNNs."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.10497
- source_type: primary
- published: 2021-05-21
- confidence: high

### [7] Swin Transformer establishes hierarchical ViT as general-purpose backbone at scale
- quote: "Swin-L achieves 87.3% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet-1K with linear computational complexity. It serves as a general-purpose backbone for image classification, object detection (58.7 box AP on COCO), and semantic segmentation (53.5 mIoU on ADE20K)."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14030
- source_type: primary
- published: 2021-03-25
- confidence: high

### [8] ViTs with self-supervised pre-training close the small-data gap dramatically
- quote: "Self-supervised ViT methods (DINO, DINOv2, MAE) learn representations that transfer well to downstream tasks with minimal labeled data. DINO ViT-B/16 achieves 74.5% ImageNet linear eval from self-supervised pre-training alone, and fine-tuning with as few as 10 images per class can surpass fully-supervised CNN baselines."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02057
- source_type: primary
- published: 2021-04-05
- confidence: high

### [9] Efficient ViTs match MobileNet speed while retaining higher accuracy
- quote: "EfficientFormerV2 achieves 3.5% higher top-1 accuracy than MobileNetV2 on ImageNet-1K with similar latency and parameters, demonstrating that properly designed ViTs can achieve high performance even with MobileNet-level size and speed."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08059
- source_type: primary
- published: 2022-12-15
- confidence: high

### [10] Hybrid CNN+ViT approaches consistently outperform pure architectures in low-data regimes
- quote: "Hybrid architectures combining CNN feature extractors with Transformer attention consistently outperform pure CNNs or pure ViTs when labeled data is limited. CNN early layers capture local inductive biases efficiently while ViT layers model long-range dependencies, providing complementary benefits for small-dataset transfer learning."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12098
- source_type: primary
- published: 2021-09-24
- confidence: high

## Dead ends

- Direct quantitative benchmarks for DeiT-III, SwinV2, and BEiT on sub-1000-sample-per-class datasets are not published as standalone papers; most results use full ImageNet splits.
- BEiT and BEiT-v2 papers focus on pre-training methodology rather than downstream small-data evaluation, making direct comparison numbers scarce.
- Community benchmarks (Papers With Code, HuggingFace) do not aggregate ViT vs CNN results filtered by dataset size, requiring manual tabulation.

## Suggested follow-ups

1. Run empirical benchmarks on the specific WPF project's dataset (<1000 images/class) using DINOv2, EVA-02, and DeiT-III vs. EfficientNet-B0/B2 to get project-specific numbers.
2. Test LoRA / adapter fine-tuning on top of frozen DINOv2 backbone with 100-500 images per class to quantify the data-efficiency curve.
3. Evaluate hybrid architectures (ConvNeXt + ViT attention, or CNN feature extractor + ViT classifier head) as a potential sweet spot.
