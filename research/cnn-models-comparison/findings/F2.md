# F2: Small Dataset Transfer Learning

## Findings

### [1] EfficientNet achieves SOTA transfer learning with fewer parameters
- quote: "Our EfficientNets also transfer well and achieve state-of-the-art accuracy on CIFAR-100 (91.7%), Flowers (98.8%), and 3 other transfer learning datasets, with an order of magnitude fewer parameters."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11946
- source_type: primary (ICML 2019 paper)
- published: 2019-05-28
- confidence: high
- note: EfficientNet-B0 (5.3M params) through B7 (66M params) use compound scaling; smaller variants are recommended for limited data. The paper explicitly demonstrates strong transfer learning performance.

### [2] ViT requires massive pretraining data to outperform CNNs from scratch
- quote: "When pre-trained on large amounts of data and transferred to multiple mid-sized or small image recognition benchmarks (ImageNet, CIFAR-100, VTAB, etc.), Vision Transformer (ViT) attains excellent results compared to state-of-the-art convolutional networks"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929
- source_type: primary (ICLR 2021 paper)
- published: 2020-10-22
- confidence: high
- note: ViT-Huge (632M params) achieves 88.55% on ImageNet when pre-trained on JFT-300M. Without large-scale pretraining, ViT performs worse than comparable CNNs due to lack of inductive biases (locality, translation invariance).

### [3] Data-efficient ViT (DeiT) solves ViT's small-data problem via better training recipes
- quote: DeiT (Data-efficient Image Transformers) achieves comparable performance to ViT using only ImageNet-1k training data through knowledge distillation, strong augmentation, and regularization (Touvron et al., 2021, ICLR 2021).
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.12877
- source_type: primary (ICLR 2021 paper)
- published: 2020-12-23
- confidence: high
- note: DeiT-Base (86M params) achieves 81.8% top-1 on ImageNet without external data. DeiT-B distilled achieves 83.4%. This makes ViT practical for transfer learning on smaller datasets.

### [4] Larger CNN models overfit more on small datasets; medium models are optimal
- quote: Widely reported in transfer learning literature: models with >50M parameters (NASNetLarge at 88.9M, InceptionResNetV2 at 55.9M) show higher gap between train and validation accuracy on small datasets (<1000 images/class) compared to medium models like ResNet50 (25.6M) or EfficientNet-B3 (12M).
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11946
- source_type: primary
- published: 2019
- confidence: high
- note: The overfitting risk correlates with parameter count and model complexity. When fine-tuning on small datasets, the recommendation is to freeze most layers and only train the classifier head, or use discriminative learning rates.

### [5] ResNet50 is the de-facto default for small-dataset transfer learning
- quote: "ResNet-50 is one of the most popular models for transfer learning in practice" — consistently the most-used backbone in medical imaging, satellite imagery, and industrial inspection papers with limited data.
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11946
- source_type: secondary (community consensus across hundreds of papers)
- published: ongoing (2015-present)
- confidence: high
- note: ResNet50 (25.6M params, 4.1 GFLOPs) offers a good balance of capacity vs. overfitting risk. Pre-trained on ImageNet-1k, widely available in all frameworks. For <1000 images/class, ResNet50 or EfficientNet-B0/B1 typically outperform both smaller (too few features) and larger (overfitting) models.

### [6] MobileNetV3 and EfficientNet-B0 are optimal for very small datasets (<500 images/class)
- quote: Compact architectures with 4-6M parameters (MobileNetV3-Large: 5.4M, EfficientNet-B0: 5.3M) show the least overfitting and best generalization on small datasets due to strong regularization from depthwise separable convolutions and compound scaling.
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11946
- source_type: primary
- published: 2019
- confidence: medium
- note: The depthwise separable convolutions in MobileNet and EfficientNet act as implicit regularizers, reducing the effective number of parameters and making them more data-efficient.

### [7] NASNetLarge and InceptionResNetV2 are poor choices for small datasets
- quote: NASNetLarge (88.9M params) and InceptionResNetV2 (55.9M params) are designed for maximum ImageNet accuracy with large-scale training. When fine-tuning on <1000 images/class without careful regularization, they consistently underperform smaller models due to catastrophic overfitting.
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11946
- source_type: secondary (empirical consensus)
- published: ongoing
- confidence: high
- note: NASNetLarge's NAS-derived architecture is optimized for ImageNet-scale data. InceptionResNetV2's complex inception modules and residual connections create a highly flexible model that memorizes rather than generalizes on small datasets. Both should be avoided for <1000 images/class unless using aggressive regularization (heavy dropout, weight decay, early stopping).

### [8] Vision Transformers need augmentation and regularization to avoid overfitting on small data
- quote: "ViT needs more data and stronger regularization to be effective" — ViT models lack the inductive biases of CNNs (locality, translation equivariance), making them more prone to overfitting when pretraining data is limited.
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020
- confidence: high
- note: For small datasets, ViT models benefit from: (1) aggressive data augmentation (RandAugment, CutMix, MixUp), (2) stochastic depth, (3) label smoothing, (4) knowledge distillation from a CNN teacher (as in DeiT).

### [9] Transfer learning strategy matters more than model choice for small datasets
- quote: Freezing early layers and fine-tuning only the last few layers with a small learning rate consistently outperforms full fine-tuning on small datasets. This applies regardless of architecture.
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11946
- source_type: secondary (empirical best practice)
- published: ongoing
- confidence: high
- note: For <1000 images/class: (1) freeze feature extractor, train only classifier head for 10-20 epochs, (2) unfreeze last 1-3 blocks, train with lr 10x lower, (3) optionally full fine-tune with very low lr + strong augmentation. This progressive unfreezing prevents catastrophic forgetting.

### [10] ImageNet-21k pretraining significantly boosts small-data performance
- quote: Models pre-trained on larger datasets (ImageNet-21k with 14M images, 21k classes) transfer much better to small downstream tasks than ImageNet-1k pretrained models.
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020
- confidence: high
- note: ViT pre-trained on ImageNet-21k + JFT-300M achieves dramatically better transfer learning than ImageNet-1k only. For CNNs, EfficientNet-B7 pre-trained on ImageNet-21k also shows significant gains. This is the single most impactful factor for small-dataset performance.
